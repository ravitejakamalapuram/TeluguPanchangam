Five rules

  1. The panchangam is the hero; the clock is a detail.
  2. Answer "is now a good time?" in one glance, with a word and an icon, never colour alone.
  3. One language at a time. No "తిథి (TITHI)".
  4. Daily reading first; tools (sankalpam, reminders, calendar) one tap away.
  5. Calm, warm, legible. No neon, glow loops or heavy blur.

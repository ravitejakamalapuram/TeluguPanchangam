# Revenue and marketing operations: who does what, and when

Owner: Claude (the "Marketing plan for three apps" thread in the git-personal project).
Raviteja's part is limited to what only he can do: post from his own accounts, click in store
dashboards, approve prices, and merge PRs. Everything else is prepared for him, and he is
reminded at the right time.

## How Claude keeps this moving

| When | What happens | Where it reaches Raviteja |
|---|---|---|
| Evening before each launch-kit post (15 Oct 2026 to 11 Apr 2027) | The routine drafts the post, rechecks the dates and fills in the calendar reminder | Thread card plus a 9:00 AM IST calendar reminder |
| Every Monday, 9:00 AM IST | Weekly owner brief: the top three things that need him this week, anything overdue, and a request for the week's Chrome Web Store user counts | One message in the thread |
| When a PR needs a merge or a store step needs a click | One ask, with the link | The thread |

A reminder that gets no answer is raised again in the next Monday brief until it is done or
dropped. Nothing is posted publicly in his name.
